### 🔐 TFG Project: Zero Trust Infrastructure Lab

My final degree project is a full deployment of a segmented Zero Trust network architecture using:
- 🐳 **Docker** (isolated service containers)
- 🌐 **GNS3** (network topology and routing simulation)
- 🔒 **Keycloak** (identity and access control)
- 📜 **OPA** (fine-grained policy enforcement)
- 🧠 **Wazuh** (SIEM + log aggregation)
- 🔍 **Suricata** & **Zeek** (network threat detection)
- 💣 **Kali Linux** (Red Team attack simulations)

> The goal is to evaluate how a ZTA model can prevent, detect, and respond to attacks like port scanning, unauthorized access, and lateral movement.
